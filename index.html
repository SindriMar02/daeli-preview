Our family’s place

Welcoming guests since 1988.

We started welcoming guests to Dæli in 1988, and our goal has always been to make every stay special.

Kiddi and Haffi run the farm with their son Bolli and their daughter Þórhildur, and a team of helping hands. Everyone here speaks English as well as Icelandic.

Getting here

Six kilometres off Road 1.

Turn south off the Ring Road onto Víðidalsvegur, road 715, and follow it up the valley to the farm.

In every house

Every house is self-catering, with its own kitchen corner and its own shower room.

  • Garden and river views
  • Free parking at the houses
  • A cot on request, free for children under three
  • Check-in 15:00 to 22:00, check-out by 11:00
  • No pets, and no smoking
